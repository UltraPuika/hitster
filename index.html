Front: QR code. Back: artist, title and the year in big numbers. Sheets are A4, laid out for double-sided printing.

Double-sided printing

The back pages are mirrored so every back lands behind its own front. In the print dialog choose A4, scale 100% (no "fit to page"), double-sided with the same flip setting. Print the test sheet first and hold it against the light.

Sheets to include

The print file is a web page. Open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox and press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P), then "Save as PDF" or print. Text stays sharp and the QR codes are drawn as vectors.

Add or update songs

Paste a CSV (comma, semicolon or tab separated) or JSON, or choose a file. Songs are added to the local list; a song with the same Spotify track is updated. Nothing is deleted.

Exported from Spotify (e.g. with Exportify)? Paste that CSV as it is. Song links and years are read from it. Years from Spotify are marked check because they can be a remaster or compilation date.

Own columns: card_id, artist, title, year, release_date, genre, language, spotify_url, spotify_track_id, alt_url, source, note (language must be lv)

Song list

Songs come from data/songs.json in the repository. Edits, additions and deletions are saved in this browser. To publish them, export the songs (JSON) and replace data/songs.json.
